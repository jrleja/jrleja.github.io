We applied this new *Prospector-α* model to a mass-complete sample of 58,461 galaxies
across 0.5 < z < 2.5 from the 3D-HST survey. These new inferences lowered the observed
cosmic star formation rate density by ∼0.2 dex and increased the observed stellar mass
growth by ∼0.1 dex, finally bringing these two measurements into agreement and implying
an older, more quiescent Universe than found in previous work.
