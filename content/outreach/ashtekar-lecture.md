I gave an [Ashtekar Frontiers of Science 2024 Lecture](https://science.psu.edu/frontiers/joel-leja-michael-eracleous) at Penn State, entitled “Surprises at the Dawn of Time from James Webb: A First Look at the First Stars, Galaxies, and Black Holes”, with an intro from Prof. Michael Eracleous. This was an exciting opportunity to share cutting-edge research from the James Webb Space Telescope to the Penn State community, with over 200 attendees!
